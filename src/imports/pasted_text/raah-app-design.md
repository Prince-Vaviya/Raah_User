Design a premium, modern mobile application called "Raah", an AI-powered smart public transport platform for commuters.

The design language should feel like a combination of Uber, Google Maps, Citymapper, Linear, Apple Human Interface Guidelines, and Notion.

Avoid government application aesthetics.

The experience should feel calm, intelligent, trustworthy, premium and effortless.

Use soft gradients, floating cards, rounded corners, generous white space, subtle shadows, glassmorphism where appropriate, smooth hierarchy and minimal clutter.

Design for iPhone 16 Pro (393 × 852 px).
